COACHES SPOTLIGHT.

Meet the coaches and team captains who help our Storm Family lead, learn, and grow.

Founder & Head Coach

Coach Brittany Taylor

Hello, my name is Brittany Taylor. I am a proud graduate of J. H. Rose High School Class of 2010 — Go Rampants! — and a Fall 2016 graduate of Elizabeth City State University — Viking Pride! I currently serve as a 4th grade teacher at Lakeforest Elementary School and a proud native and resident of Greenville. I am also the founder of the Blue Storm Step Team (BSST, established in 2023) and Khoatic Kat3gory 3 (KK3, established in 2025). In addition, I am a member of Krimson Kourts Incorporated and the proud mother of one handsome son. I have been stepping since the 3rd grade and have been a part of several step teams throughout the years, including the co-ed step team Rated X at Elizabeth City State University. I am also a proud alumna of the Marching Sound of Class at ECSU. During my time there, I helped create the name “En-V,” formerly used by the ECSU SOC Color Guard, and I later served as both a member and captain of En-V. “I saw a void that needed to be filled. The town my students live in does not provide enough positive outlets and activities for children, especially within the school setting. I wanted to give these kids something meaningful — something that teaches lifelong skills, builds confidence, creates opportunities, and helps guide them toward success.” Line Name: InKuisitive Insight Line Number: 3 Line: Kuarantine Khaos

💙💜 WELCOME TO THE STORM FAMILY! 💜💙

💙💜 WELCOME TO THE STORM FAMILY! TWO TEAMS. ONE FAMILY. ONE STORM. 💜💙 WELCOME, STORM & KAT3GORY 3 MEMBERS AND PARENTS! 🎉 First and foremost, CONGRATULATIONS! We are beyond excited to welcome you to the **Blue Storm Step Team (BSST) and Khaotic Kat3gory 3 Step Team (KK3) family! You are joining something much bigger than a step team. You're becoming part of a family built on LOVE, UNITY, DISCIPLINE, DEDICATION, and PURPOSE! HOW THE STORM BEGAN: Founded by Coach Brittany Taylor, Blue Storm Step Team was established in 2023, followed by Khaotic Kat3gory 3 in 2025. With a passion for stepping that began in the third grade and continued through her years at Elizabeth City State University, Coach Taylor wanted to create a positive outlet where young people could discover their talents, build confidence, develop leadership skills, and feel a sense of belonging. Our teams were founded to give children something positive to be part of, help them channel negative energy into positive actions, and encourage them to make choices that lead toward success. OUR PURPOSE: BIGGER THAN STEPPING! Our mission goes far beyond performances and competitions. We strive to develop confident, disciplined, respectful, and responsible young leaders through teamwork, mentorship, academic excellence, cultural awareness, and community involvement. We're not just building talented steppers. We're building future leaders and changemakers! 💙💜 TO OUR PARENTS & MEMBERS: To our members, get ready to work hard, make new friends, challenge yourselves, and create unforgettable memories! Remember, you don't have to be perfect, but you must be willing to learn, grow, and give your best! To our parents, thank you for trusting us with your children. Your support, encouragement, and involvement mean everything. Together, we will create a safe, loving, and positive environment where every child has the opportunity to SHINE! LET THE JOURNEY BEGIN! We cannot wait to see the growth, talent, friendships, and accomplishments this season will bring! Welcome to a family where we don't just make noise… WE MAKE AN IMPACT! With love and excitement, Coach Brittany Taylor Founder & Head Coach 💙 Blue Storm Step Team | Est. 2023 💜 Khaotic Kat3gory 3 | Est. 2025 💙💜💙💜💙💜
